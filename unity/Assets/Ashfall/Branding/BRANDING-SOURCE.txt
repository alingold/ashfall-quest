# ASHFALL branding v1 — report

**Result:** ASHFALL now has an original identity: the **Fissure A** symbol, a custom-drawn
wordmark, four lockups in four colourways, every raster the game and store need, a UI theme
(THEME.md) with measured contrast and colour-blind-checked team colours, and seven mock-ups of
the restyled menus. Everything regenerates from vector sources with one command, and an
automated check passes on the final files.

## The chosen concept

**Fissure A — the ember in the fault.** Two ash-white blades form a peak. The right blade
carries the summit; the left stops at a fine fault line. Where an A's crossbar would be, an
ember glows. It is the game's name made visual: rock, ash, and an ember still alive inside.

Why it beat the other four directions (critique in `explore/NOTES.md`, sheet in `explore/concepts.png`):
- **It reads at icon sizes.** It is a recognisable A and peak down to 16 px. At 48 px the
  fissure is 2 px and the ember 7 px wide, with 2 px clearance.
- **Symbol and word share one construction.** The wordmark's two A's *are* the symbol at text
  weight, so the mark, the wordmark and the UI's diamond motif form one system.
- **It works physically.** It is three islands with no enclosed counters, so it stencils in one
  cut, stamps, and embroiders as two blades plus a diamond.
- **It moves.** The ember ignites and sheds embers upward while ash falls: the title-moment
  motif.
- The rejected directions failed on specific grounds. The caldera ring reads as a power button.
  The strata peak and plume-A are stock shapes or tropes. The falling ember breaks up at 48 px.

**Palette.** Charcoal `#14161B` boards, ash-white `#ECE7DF` text, ember `#FF6A2B` and hot ember
`#FFB15C` as the only accent, teal `#39C6B8` for information, and danger/success as functional
colours. Body text measures 7.1–15.4:1 on every surface. Team colours: Ember/Glacier
`#FF8A3D`/`#3E9BFF` by default, with Sulfur/Violet `#F2C230`/`#9A6BFF` as the alternate. Both
pairs stay at least 47 ΔE apart under protan, deutan and tritan simulation.

**Tagline (proposal):** "THE FRONTIER IS VERTICAL". It appears in the hero art and the title moment.

## How the mock-ups stay honest
They use only what `R23VrUi` draws today: flat quads, vertical gradients from per-vertex
colours (`Quad()` already writes vertex colours, and the shader multiplies by `_Color`),
45°-rotated quads for diamonds, textured quads for thumbnails, icons and the logo PNG, and the
shipped font at its single weight with no letter spacing. Every interactive element sits at the
coordinates in `R23VrUI*.cs`/`R24*Ui.cs`. The restyle adds non-interactive backdrops, header
bands, frames and diamonds, and moves page headings up 16 px into the band.

## Font and licences
- **UI font:** `R23InventoryUI.ttf` = Open Sans SemiBold, © 2020 The Open Sans Project Authors,
  SIL Open Font License 1.1 (licence file next to it, and recorded in the font's name table).
  It also contains one merged Noto Sans Symbols 2 glyph (OFL). The family name was changed to
  "Ashfall Inventory UI", which complies with the OFL. The mock-ups and the hero tagline embed
  this file; nothing else typographic is used.
- **Logo:** custom paths drawn in Python from a 45°-cut construction. No font outlines, stock
  icons or third-party art. The hero scene and all textures are procedural.
- **Map thumbnails in the mock** are the project's own Range/Valley/Cyber Bird images, read
  only. Paintball's archive thumbnail is third-party, so the mock shows the missing-map card
  instead.

## How to regenerate
```
python tools\build.py
```
This runs from `authoring\branding-v1` in about 35 s. It writes `explore/`, `logo/`, `export/`,
`mock/` and `THEME.md`, then runs `tools\validate.py`. Requirements: Python 3.13 with Pillow
(12.0.0 was used; it is already installed globally, so no venv was needed) and Microsoft Edge,
used headless as the rasteriser with a throw-away profile per call. Edit colours and motion in
`tools/theme.py`, letterforms in `tools/glyphs.py`, and the symbol in `tools/marks.py`.

**Validation on the final files:**
- 56 SVGs: paths only, every subpath closed, no text, images or external references. All
  artwork contours are simple, with no self-intersections or duplicate points.
- 14 exports at exact sizes; transparent files have a transparent background, and opaque files
  are saved without alpha.
- The adaptive foreground stays inside the 66 dp circle.
- All 23 text pairings meet their targets, and THEME.md's JSON equals the generator.
- Directory size is 7 MB, and no Edge process is left running.

- Use `export/app-icon-1024.png`, the two adaptive layers and `export/splash-logo.png` as the

- THEME.md §9 maps each of the survey's inline hex colours to a token. §5–6 define the header
  band and button/card states using the existing 6 mm `Button underline` quad.
- Quad sort order comes from quad names. New backdrops must contain "backdrop" in their names.

## Open questions and uncertainties
1. **Trademark clearance** has not been done. A-shaped peak marks are common in games. Ours
   differs by construction (open two-blade peak with a fault and a lit ember, no solid
   silhouette, not red), but a professional search is needed before commercial release.
2. **Headset checks** cannot be done off-engine:
   - legibility of the new 17 px minimum captions;
   - whether the subtle `panel-top`→`panel-bottom` vertex gradient bands on Quest (if it does,
     flatten it to `panel`);
   - whether the 2 px hairlines alias at 1.6 m;
   - whether the title moment's ember count feels right.
3. **Team set A's team 1 (`#FF8A3D`) equals the `primary` button colour.** This is fine in the
   HUD, but a menu roster swatch beside START MATCH would look related. Use the diamond swatch
   shape there, or switch to set B.
4. **Store art:** only the two requested hero sizes were made. Meta's store asks for further
   aspect ratios; `tools/exports.py` `hero()` can emit any size from the same scene.
5. **Match-rules layout** (pre-existing): the explanation line at y = 228 sits close to the
   first rule row at y = 258. It is legible in the mock, but worth checking in the headset.
6. **Outfit mock** uses placeholder names and figures; the new original skins had no
   thumbnails yet.
7. The tagline is a proposal and the owner may replace it. The title moment falls back to
   TextMesh if the PNG is absent.
8. The general report-branding rule in the user's report guidelines (U of U ECE logo) was not
   applied. This is the game's own identity, not a report.
