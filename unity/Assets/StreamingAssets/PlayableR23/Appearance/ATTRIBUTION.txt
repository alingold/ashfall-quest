ASHFALL Originals - player skins

All 24 player skins in this folder (meshes and textures) are original procedural
characters authored for ASHFALL (2026-11). Source specs and generators:
ashfall/authoring/skins-v1. Dedicated to the project; no third-party geometry or textures.

Articulated VR hand / glove base mesh and UV layout (preview-menu-left/right.meshbin and
HandsWeapons/left|right.meshbin): Godot XR Tools "Hand_Glove_low" by DigitalN8m4r3
(Miodrag Sejic), CC0-1.0 (assets) / MIT (repository),
https://github.com/GodotVR/godot-xr-tools. Each skin paints its glove material on that UV.

The previous 115 skins were recreations of other games' characters and were retired to
ashfall/retired/20261110-recreated-skins. PBAN, OBSIDIAN and WITCH DOCTOR (Claude Opus)
are recreations restored at the owner's request. Builds containing them are for private use;
the other 112 remain retired.
