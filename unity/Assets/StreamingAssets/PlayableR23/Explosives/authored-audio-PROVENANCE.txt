ASHFALL ORIGINAL SOUND BANK
51 mono PCM WAV assets, 32kHz / 16bit.
Created for this project by deterministic procedural sound design. No third-party recordings, performances, copyrighted song excerpts, or external sample packs.
Reproducible source: scripts/audio_bank.gd synthesize(kind,variant).
Baker: tools/bake_audio.gd in workspace. Run Godot --headless --audio-driver Dummy --xr-mode off --path AshfallVR --script <absolute tools/bake_audio.gd>.
Layers include filtered random excitation, damped resonant modes, timed mechanical transients, gas-body decay and discrete early reflections. Environmental loops use endpoint fades.
These are designed synthetic assets, not live recordings of real firearms. Auditory realism and headset balance require later listening review; none was performed while the user rested.
Numerical audit: reports/audio-bank-audit.json. Peak<=0.78 full scale before in-game gain. No clipped PCM samples.
