# Native motion outcome v1 — additive candidate policy

Root design decision, 2026-11-04. This policy does not enable legacy HostMotionArbiter calls, approve native execution, or close H2/H3. It authorizes an additive concrete owner implementation and staged validation, preserving the existing unsupported-path refusals.

Unity CharacterController.Move resolves displacement during the call. An exact no-fail physical result cannot be promised by a prior generic sweep, caller flag or callback. Therefore an alternative additive path may represent irreversible publication explicitly rather than returning an ordinary rejection after movement.

Before the first physical write, the actual owner must validate and reserve its exact controller/root/hull, proposal, source frame/generation and local revision, controller enabled state, finite destination and prepared mechanics. All available admission/geometry checks and allocations must occur before writes. Canonical Session/life/connection authority remains root-owned and cannot be inferred from caller-supplied counters. Unsupported authority or geometry must refuse before the first write.

After any physical write, the operation must retain an immutable owner-created result containing exact physical and pivot deltas, collision flags, resulting root pose, attempted proposal, and opaque operation identity. It must report Applied or AppliedPendingAccounting, never NotApplied/Rejected. Partial pivot or interrupted phases remain explicit. There is no transform rewind, second gravity solve, or replay of a completed physical phase. One operation remains outstanding per actual body; subsequent motion is refused until exact idempotent accounting completes or the owner is terminally retired. Disposal/rejection cannot erase the retained irreversible result.

Accounting is an explicit consumer step over the retained receipt and the actual prepared mechanics. It must not call the physical solver again. No callback or boolean may manufacture its success. A full integrated path needs actual Player/Flight/climb/capsule/pad participants, retained pending receipts, authoritative lifecycle and crash dispositions. A private isolated receipt prototype is insufficient for that full integration claim.

The additive typed path must remain separate from legacy IExclusivePlayerMotor.Apply/RuleResult paths until all consumers deliberately adopt these outcomes. Existing callers must not silently treat a physically applied incomplete result as a rollback or ordinary rejection. Old preservation counterexamples remain evidence until enabled complete composition and independent/native tests pass. Real source collision behavior, angular/offset/moving geometry and human motion feel remain separate gates. Candidate04 desktop build is immutable and unaffected.
