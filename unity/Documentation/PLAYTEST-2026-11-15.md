# Playtest 2026-11-15 (host al + guest pban, two Quests, Unity Relay rooms)

Build tested: `1.3.10-playtest-r11` (non-polished). Fix branch: `playtest-r12` (from baseline `33140cb0`).
Evidence (outside the repo): `~/ashfall/playtest-20261115/`
- `mp3_transcript.txt`: 57-min voice call (call clock).
- `quest.mp4`: host headset recording. Video clock is about call clock minus 17:00.
- `frames/`, `sheets2/`: one frame per 20 s, with video-time labels.

What worked: Relay room create/join by code, both players visible, damage, death, respawn, ghost state, scoreboard, timer.

## Issues (ID, what was seen, where in the evidence)

### P0: multiplayer blockers
- **MP-PERF**: In multiplayer, frame rate collapses to about 1 fps ("slideshow") on Battlefield Paintball, Badlands and Magnum. It gets worse over time. It does not happen on Sandbox Range, in single-player, or after the host leaves. Host is worse than guest. Call 15:00-16:30, 19:10-22:50, 34:50-35:30.
- **MP-FLOATING-LOOT**: On Magnum, lots of backpacks and objects float in the sky. They are visible from far away and all render; the host thinks this is a perf cause. Request: stop drawing or loading world objects beyond about 40 m. Call 39:57-40:57, 54:21.
- **ROOM-MAPCHANGE**: When the host picks a new map (Map Select, the Match Rules "Lobby" button, or Home), clients are not brought along. Host and guest end up in different rooms, and the host must make a new room code every time. Expected: the room survives map changes, all members follow the host, and Home returns everyone to the room lobby together. Call 22:45-23:40, 32:48-34:24, 35:52-36:17, 51:35-52:01. Video 34:20 and 38:20 show "LOCAL OFFLINE" in Match Rules while the host thought it was hosting.
- **MP-PICKUP**: In multiplayer, players often cannot pick up guns, backpacks or the grapple gun. "Only one player can pick up things." This worked in single-player. Later on Magnum, pickups partly worked. Request: grey out items that can't be picked up. Also "can't pick up planes" (vehicle). Call 25:12-28:23, 30:48-31:23, 35:28.

### P1: multiplayer and menu correctness
- **MP-HITMARKER**: Damage to remote players lands, but the shooter sees no hit markers. Call 20:56-21:08, 31:23.
- **MP-RETICLE**: The reticle doesn't snap or scale to the distance of a remote player, as it does for other targets. Call 44:59-45:46.
- **ROOM-APPEARANCE**: The host picked the Witch Doctor skin, but creating the room reset it to the orange Cyclops skin. Players cannot change appearance or player model while in a room lobby. Host and guests should both be able to. Call 23:37-24:37, 38:14-38:51.
- **ROOM-LOBBY-MENU**: The room lobby panel lacks the main-menu buttons (Outfits, Settings, Map Select for the host). Call 24:37-24:53.
- **MENU-HOME-MISCLICK**: Both players repeatedly pressed HOME trying to close the pause menu, which dropped them out of the room. Add a Back/Close button at the bottom of the pause menu (Population One habit), and require confirmation before Home leaves a room or match. Call 18:03-18:31, 43:31-43:44, 47:32-48:09.
- **MENU-MATCH-LABELS**: The pause menu says "Restart Round", Match Rules says "Start Round" or "Start Match". When a match is running, Match Rules should say "End Match". The two menus should offer the same actions with the same labels. Guests should not get host-only controls (Restart Round, Map Select). Call 30:34-30:48, 31:47-32:05, 41:38-41:54.
- **MENU-START-TWICE**: Start Match sometimes needs two presses. Once, Start Round dropped both players back to the main menu before the match started. Call 30:40-30:48, 52:45-52:53.
- **MAP-PODS-MAGNUM**: Team Revive on Magnum used drop pods. Magnum should spawn players inside the arena, without pods. Pods should be decided per map. Call 48:37-49:44.
- **MAP-SPAWN-MARKERS**: Spawn-point markers or figures are visible on Magnum and should be hidden. Video 27:40 and 30:00. Call 44:11.
- **MAP-JUMPPADS-MAGNUM**: Launch and jump pads don't work on Magnum (they work on other maps). Call 39:16-39:32, 44:10.
- **MP-BOTS**: On the map with bots, no bots appeared in the multiplayer session (mode "Lobby exploration" or Team Revive). The players were unsure whether Practice or Explore would spawn them. Call 55:00-55:27.
- **WPN-CX4-SEMI**: In multiplayer the CX4 fires only one bullet per trigger pull, as if semi-automatic. Call 29:11-29:16.
- **FLIGHT-DIVE**: In dive mode, pushing forward accelerates far too fast. Falling feels like full gravity; Population One has more air resistance. Call 53:10-54:21.
- **UI-MAP-PANEL**: The left-trigger map panel should show each player's team, and it should not move with the head. Call 50:23-50:49.

## r12 headset session (2026-11-15 evening; host al on Quest r12 1.3.11-playtest-r12, PC NetBot guest via Relay)
Evidence:

- Quest FPS log: `~/ashfall/mp-rig-runs/quest-fps-GMWDTK.log`, VrApi per second.
- Bot log: `~/ashfall/mp-rig-runs/20261115-201124-room-GMWDTK/`.

### r12b issues
- **WIN2 (P0):** On Badlands the player still cannot run through orange-framed windows. In the video, they push their hand through the opening, are pushed back, and repeat. The scripted bot passes, so a real headset player (real head height, head hull/exclusion, body column) does not. Population One lets you run or vault straight through window openings.
- **GPU-PERF (P0):** Badlands match on Quest 3, host plus one guest: 55-67 fps average, dips to 28-35. GPU 87-97% busy, CPU 27-37%, GPU clock 456 MHz at perf level `CPU4/GPU=2/2`. GPU-bound: needs foveation (the polish branch had SRP foveation 0.5), higher CPU/GPU performance levels, and lower full-screen costs.
- **PICKUP2 (P0):** As host, the player cannot pick up ground weapons (CX4, sword) even with an empty inventory slot. Backpacks and a flashbang work. After another backpack, one more weapon can be taken: "I just can't fill that last backpack slot". Off-by-one between the UI slots and host capacity?
- **REMOTE (P0):** The remote player shows no weapon in hand, makes no shot sound, and draws no tracers. Damage numbers do appear. The bot died twice (bot log t=374 s, 621 s) but the host did not perceive it dying or taking damage; check remote death and ghost presentation.
- **WHEELDROP:** In the weapon wheel, holding A while pointing at a weapon for about 5 s should drop it, with a circular progress ring around the weapon. This existed in the Godot version.
- **AVATAR2:**
  - Wrists still don't look bendable.
  - On the menu pedestal, the arms rotate strangely when the player rotates.
  - Reduce the pedestal rotation rate by 50%.
  - Add a mirror in SANDBOX RANGE to check skins.
- **MAPPANEL2:** The left-trigger map vibrates while moving and is anchored to the hand. Anchor it relative to the body (not the head, not the hand).
- **TRAILS2:** The trails look good, but they should show only when gliding (flying) or diving, not when simply falling.
- **TEAMREVIVE-HUD:** In Team Revive, remove the kill counter. Kills don't matter; the round ends when one team eliminates the other.
- **MATCH-MUSIC:** Play music or a sting when a match starts, so the match is clearly distinct from the lobby.

## r14 full-lobby session (2026-11-16 evening; host al on Quest r14 1.3.13-playtest-r14, 7 PC NetBots BOT 1-7 via Relay, room 8CWKRH, Battlefield Paintball DM and Team Revive)
Evidence: `~/ashfall/playtest-20261115/r14-session/quest-fps-full-lobby-202540.log` (VrApi per second); bot logs `~/ashfall/mp-rig-runs/20261116-202758-fill-8CWKRH/bot1..7/`.
Frame rate with 8 players: the user called it good. Over 24 min the average was 58 fps (median 60, p10 44) and App GPU time was 15.3 ms; GPU-bound. Do not tune rendering unless asked.

### r14 issues
- **STARTUP-LOGO:** the static ASHFALL loading logo must also show while loading at first app startup, not only on map loads.
- **MAPCHANGE-DIRECT:** a host map change sends everyone through the menu map before the next map. Go straight to the next map; the menu map is only for before the first game.
- **WARMUP-SPAWNS (P0):** on Paintball, in both Deathmatch and Team Revive, warmup spawned everyone at the normal game spawns instead of the warmup spawn area on top of the map. When the match started, players were not sent to the game spawns. Maps have separate warmup and match spawn sets; use them.
- **TEAM-SPAWNS (P0):** teams must spawn on opposite sides of the map. Today everyone spawned in the same spot regardless of team (sometimes mixed across one side or both sides).
- **HEALTH-STATION-MP:** the health (regen) stations do not work in multiplayer.
- **HEALTH-RING-ZORDER:** the health-station timer ring draws behind the scoreboards; it should draw in front.
- **SHIELD-STACKS:** every ground shield is actually a stack of two shields; it should be one.
- **WHEELDROP2:** holding A in the weapon wheel over a shield, the shield shaker and probably other non-gun items does not drop them; it should work as it does for guns.
- **BACKPACK-GRIP-DROP:** in the backpack during a match, dropping items with the grip button does not work.
- **PICKUP4 (P0):** the host cannot hold more than three guns. With two open backpack slots, nothing can be picked up. Taking another backpack allows one more pickup, but the remaining two slots still can't be filled. PICKUP2/3 did not fix this.
- **TEAM-NAMETAGS:** you can't tell teammates from enemies. Enemies get no name tag. Teammates get a name tag plus health and shield bars.
- **MAP-TEAM-ONLY:** the tactical map shows only your own teammates, never the other team.
- **MAP-DEAD-ALIVE:** dead and alive players need different markers on the map.
- **REVIVE-MP (P0):** in Team Revive the host could not revive a downed teammate bot (blue, so same team) by holding still next to it.
- **BOT-AIM:** NetBots should target and shoot at players on the other team instead of firing randomly.
- **MATCH-END:** at match end, play music. If you won, show confetti. Show your placement (1st, 2nd ...), kills and deaths. The celebration lasts 10 s, then everyone returns to the warmup spawn points.
- **SPR-SCOPE:** the SPR scope needs more magnification. Match the Godot version exactly; the author tuned it there (2x, 3x or 4x; read it from the Godot source).
- **MAP-HOLD-ITEM:** opening the map with the left trigger hides the held gun or item. You should keep holding it and be able to shoot while the map is open.
- **SWORD-VANISH:** the sword disappears when it touches map geometry (old bug, never fixed).
- **SWORD-HITREG:** the sword is unreliable: many misses and no hit markers, especially against a target standing against a wall.
- **GRAVITY2:** a smidge more gravity than r14 (12).
- **FLY-CLIFF (P0, testable):** run off a cliff or a tall structure with arms out to the sides: you should fly (glide) immediately. Today it often doesn't. Gliding is still unreliable.
