## TEAMS2

  - assignment, spawn sides and team colours/labels;
  - tactical-map roster and team visibility;
  - scoring, match end and the revive rules;
  - bots.
- **TEAMS-ASSIGN (queued):** the game auto-balances when the count changes or a player joins. Then the host (only the host) can move any player to another team from the room menu. Players do not pick their own team.
- **PODS-SHARED (queued):** never more than 2 drop-pod sky platforms. With 3 or more teams, the teams share the two platforms (12 seats each, 24 players maximum).
- **PLATFORM-UNARMED:** "players have no guns or inventory items until they leave platform."
  - Now that enemies can share a platform, nobody may hold or use a gun, sword, fists, grenade, grapple, backpack item or wheel item on the platform. The host must also reject any damage involving a player who is on the platform.
  - Item input is already blocked while `Rig.CarrierActive` (R23PlayableLoop.cs:1134). Check everything that block does not cover.
- The team-count slider limits are a MODES item (TEAMS-MIN). Coordinate through requests.

## MOVE
- **FLY-THRESHOLD:**
  1. Remove the hysteresis. The stop-flying threshold must equal the start-flying threshold, by moving the cancel threshold OUT to the start threshold (not the start threshold in). Then a slight hand movement switches flying on and off quickly.
  2. Then move the shared threshold 4 inches (10.16 cm) further out, so the hands must be 4 in further away to fly.
  - Reason: a sideways fling off a wall accidentally started flying, which cancelled the fling (expected behaviour, wrong threshold).
  - Use the seated headset-realism options for evidence.
- **TERMINAL-VELOCITY:** increase the falling terminal velocity. The author gave no number.
  - Find the current value and any Population: One reference (r12 FLIGHT, r13 FLY3 and r15 FLY4 reports).
  - Choose a clearly higher value and record the reason.
- **FALL-FAILSAFE:** if a player falls more than 100 m below the lowest geometry of the current map, recover them:
  - Team Revive match: the player is dead (eliminated as if killed).
  - TDM and FFA: they respawn alive at a normal spawn above the map.
  - Warm-up: they respawn alive at a warm-up spawn.
  - In rooms the host decides.
  - Seen on Thor City (below the map at match start, "perpetually falling").
- **TACMAP-PLACE:** the left-trigger tactical map should appear 2 in (5 cm) lower, with the same follow logic as the weapon selector:
  - when opened, it appears at the same place relative to the head;
  - then it follows the body (left-stick locomotion and turning), not head movement.
