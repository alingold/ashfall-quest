# Vehicle seat controls and protocol (r19, VEHICLES19)

## Controls
- **Eject handle.** Every vehicle seat (hover scooter, helicopter, tank) has a glowing overhead bar 30 cm above and 12 cm in front of the seated head, in rig space. The anchor is captured when boarding ends, re-centres slowly (4 s time constant, so height recalibration is absorbed) and freezes while the handle is held, so it never follows the head. Grab: grip rising edge within 12 cm of the bar, with a free tracked hand (either hand). Pull the bar down 12 cm (max 20 cm) while holding for at least 0.1 s: ejects, with a haptic tick every 3 cm, a strong haptic and a clunk at the threshold. A brush with no grip, a stationary grip, a short pull, a grip pressed before the hand enters reach, or a hand that wanders more than 32 cm away never ejects; the bar springs back. The old head-following text prompt is gone.
- **Fallback exit.** The pre-r19 inputs remain, deliberately slower and never while the right hand is on or near the handle: hold the right grip 1.5 s (was 0.5 s, helicopter 0.6 s); on the scooter also hold jump 1.5 s. Both send an ordinary dismount (no pull marker).
- **Helicopter collective.** While the LEFT grip is held (press must be fresh, not the mounting grip and not a grip that began on the handle), the left controller's pitch relative to its pose at grip press sets vertical speed: 6 degree dead zone, linear to full at 30 degrees, smoothed (0.12 s), then the dynamics' acceleration limit. Full up +6 m/s, full down -10 m/s, neutral holds altitude. The left grip is reserved for the whole hold (no grabs, no climbing). Release returns to: X/A climbs (+6), left stick click descends fast, otherwise passive descent.
- **Rates (m/s, accel m/s^2).** Before r19: passive descent -2, click descent -6, climb +6, accel 12. After: passive -5, click -10, collective descent to -10, climb +6, accel 16. The last 2 m still arrest descent and the ground clamp is unchanged. `R25HelicopterDynamics.Step` with the old 9 arguments keeps the old rates for the maintained suites.

## Protocol (lobby version string not bumped; the lead bumps it)
- `playable.vehicle.state/1`: two floats appended after `GunPitch`: `HandlePull` (m, 0..0.20) and `Collective` (-1..1; helicopter only). Both are zero with no rider. Other republishes (aim, damage, mount) keep the rider's current values.
- `playable.vehicle.fire/1` mode 4 (guest to host): `[id][mode=4][float pull][float collective]`. Host checks rider custody and ranges, stores both and republishes the row. Guests send at most 10 Hz on change.
- `playable.vehicle.dismount/1`: optional trailing float `pull` after `yaw`. Absent = legacy exit, always handled as before. Present = handle exit: the host accepts only if both the message pull and its own recorded `HandlePull` are at least 11.95 cm. The guest sends mode 4 with the final pull immediately before it.
- Collective vertical motion is not a new input: the rider's root motion already travels in raw tracking and the host's existing track envelope (55 m/s) covers it. Collective is carried in mode 4 / the state row for remote presentation and host awareness.
