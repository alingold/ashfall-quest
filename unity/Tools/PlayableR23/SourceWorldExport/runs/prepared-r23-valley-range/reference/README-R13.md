# ASHFALL R13: moving team ghosts

The complete standalone candidate is the sibling ASHFALL-R13-Ghosts folder. Launch-VR.cmd starts PC VR; Launch-VR-Low-Poly.cmd selects lighter character models; Launch-Desktop.cmd is desktop debug. Keep the builds folder intact, including the unchanged original map PCK and ASHFALL-Map-Adapters-R10.pck. R13 uses separate settings copied unchanged from R12. Initialize remains manual.

On death, players keep their selected character and can move using the existing walking/climbing/flying controls. Teammates see a translucent grayscale version of that skin through walls, with a red name and zero-health/shield bars. Opponents see no ghost body, tag, weapon or shadow. Ghosts do not block other players or projectiles. Solid-world collision remains; through-wall visibility does not mean walking through walls.

Ghosts cannot shoot, equip weapons, pick up/drop items, build, use consumables or operate revival paddles. They can approach teammates for revival under the existing Team Revive charge, team, reach and timing rules. Deathmatch uses the existing timed respawn. Revival/respawn restores original materials, collision masks, equipment permission and normal HUD state. A compact ghost/respawn notice appears above the HUD. Falling out of bounds relocates a ghost safely without restoring health or resetting its death timers.

Self-Test-Ghosts.cmd runs three hidden XR-off processes: host ghost, teammate view and opponent view, ordinary walking, validated revival with a controlled charge, deathmatch respawn and warmup return. Self-Test-Deployment.cmd retains the R12 pod test. Self-Test-Network.ps1 -Game retains the earlier real-peer glass/pad/dual-Uzi test. No self-test launches VR.
