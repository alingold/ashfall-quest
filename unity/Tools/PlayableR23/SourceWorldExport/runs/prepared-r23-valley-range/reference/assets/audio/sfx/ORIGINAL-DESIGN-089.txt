ASHFALL standalone 089 sound design

72 original deterministic PCM sound files, authored for this project from oscillators and seeded noise. No external recordings, samples or reference-game audio were used. Existing third-party music/voice credits remain applicable to the unchanged assets they identify.

48 kHz, 16-bit mono supports positional AudioStreamPlayer3D voices. Firearm families combine short high-frequency transients, low pressure/body, mechanical cycles and staggered reflected tails. Reload cues combine latch impacts with cloth/metal movement. Construction adds a quiet irregular electrical loop, placement transients and debris breakup. Resource pickups and consumables have separate tactile cues.

Revive charge beeps use paired carriers in a 5:4 relation with a faint difference-frequency component, inspired by Tartini tones. Pitch and beep rate increase with charge; ready/discharge cues are distinct. The target charge duration is 10 seconds at rest and 5.5 seconds with energetic valid paddle motion.

Files are generated once and cached by the audio bank. Output samples have faded endpoints, no clipped samples, and controlled DC/peak levels. The build loop has a quiet boundary. SFX voices use the existing SFX bus and master peak limiter. Headset loudness, localization and subjective mix acceptance still require a playtest.
