# R18 headset validation checklist

Close ASHFALL normally before updating. Use the extracted delivery's Install-Quest.cmd with USB connected; wait for INSTALLATION_OK. It preserves app data and refuses to update a running game. Open Unknown Sources > ASHFALL R18 Quest yourself. Initialize is manual.

- [ ] Initialize in the menu. Enter Alpha via READY, return, HOST, START, take/eject/land a pod, rematch and return. Height/standing/crouching and saved settings remain consistent.
- [ ] Face Alpha's center and edge; walk/look slowly past leaves, tower edges and distant signs. Compare sharpness/stability and comfort. Native PC numbers are not Quest frame-rate evidence.
- [ ] Damage health and shield, touch a green octagon: both fill instantly. A ring/countdown lasts15s; another player cannot consume it during recharge; full health/shield does not spend it.
- [ ] Alpha tilted jump-pad chains launch along their faces; spread arms mid-ascent to glide and cancel the launch.
- [ ] In warmup AND match: build, walk/shoot through doors/windows, and break complete cardinal tower grates. Grates use metal sound; doors/glass retain their distinct sounds.
- [ ] START a Team Revive match:12pods/platform, clear front view with controls below, take any pod. Ride to real support and exit; eject midway; no sudden calibration shift.
- [ ] Enable zone: current red square and next white square on held offhand map; red patterned boundary visible high into the sky. Damage follows actual moving player/pod/eject position.
- [ ] Paintball: white-circle lobby vs ground match spawn, no sky pods. Source scoreboard is beside Welcome. MellyPop: four Megatron faces. Host a scored match and verify names/scores/time/cap; walls still occlude world scoreboards.
- [ ] At MellyPop and other WEAPONS signs: approach and find red real gun racks, grab/equip, drop/repickup; correct upright wall alignment. Inspect doors, safe floors and signs not yet matched; record map/landmark for gaps.
- [ ] Afterlife has many distributed caches; each activated site has six actual pickups. Hosted lobby symbols disappear; READY inspection retains them. Aftermath beige winding paths support you without respawn.
- [ ] Backpack grip equips shield soda/shaker into hand; trigger drops. Held weapons do not glow. Dual Uzis both exhaust finite magazines; AWP remains scoped while support grip is held and closes on release.
- [ ] Sprint/reticle/HUD/menu backdrop/laser/paddle indicators stay readable near walls; no redundant resume/mic text. Repeated enter/return does not steadily grow lag or retain previous-map geometry.
- [ ] Listen to accepted gun/paddle audio and new pod/grate/station effects. No spoken story narration.

For multiplayer with one headset, use the included Launch-Desktop.cmd as a second LAN peer. Follow R18-NETWORK-CHECKLIST.md for protocol, LAN, two-headset/live voice and true WAN tiers. Report exact map, mode, phase and nearby landmark for failures. Do not treat an offline checkmark as a physical headset pass.
